# User Research

## Problem
College students often struggle to find important notes and study materials quickly before exams.

## Research Goal
Understand how students currently access and share academic notes.

## Findings

### Interviewed Users
5 college students

### Key Insights
- Most students depend on WhatsApp groups
- Notes are difficult to organize
- Students prefer mobile apps for quick access
- Searching old PDFs is difficult

## User Needs
- Organized subject-wise notes
- Fast search feature
- Easy download option
- Upload feature for sharing notes
